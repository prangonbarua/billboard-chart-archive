Notes

Every figure here is computed from the archive at page load, not written by hand. Nothing on this page is editorial.

"Not on their chart last week" is exactly that claim and no more. A first-ever appearance and a re-entry look identical from two weeks of data, so neither is called a debut. Open any title on its chart page to see which it is.

A chart is compared against its own previous published week rather than seven days back, and a chart missing either week is left out of the counts entirely. Billboard skips weeks, and treating a skipped week as a real one is how this archive has invented data before.
