Derived, not a Billboard chart. Billboard's Hot 100 Recurrents was never published with a weekly archive, so none of this is copied from it. This applies Billboard's stated retirement rule to the Hot 100 data on this site and shows what comes out. Billboard ranked its recurrents by continued airplay and streaming after a song left the Hot 100. This archive does not hold that data, so these are ordered by last Hot 100 position, which is not the order Billboard used. Most weeks retire nobody.
